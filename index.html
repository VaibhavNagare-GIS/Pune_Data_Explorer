What today's map shows

Real, researched facts about how Pune has grown, not just a list of map layers.

Population

Pune's population grew from 2,540,069 in the 2001 census to 3,124,458 in 2011, about 23 percent in one decade. The Pune Municipal Corporation area is now home to an estimated 6.2 million people, though the next official census is still pending.

City boundary

In 2021 the state government merged 23 more villages into Pune Municipal Corporation, expanding its area from 331 to 516 square kilometres. This made Pune the largest city by area in Maharashtra, ahead of Mumbai.

Built-up land

A peer-reviewed satellite study found Pune's built-up area grew from about 116.6 to 166.9 square kilometres between 1990 and 2019, a 43 percent rise, while nearby agricultural land fell by roughly 41 percent over the same period.

Plain-language notes on what each index measures, for anyone new to satellite data.

NDVI

Normalized Difference Vegetation Index

Measures how much healthy plant growth is present. Higher values mean more green vegetation.

MNDWI

Modified Normalized Difference Water Index

Measures how much open water is present. Higher values mean more visible water.

NDBI

Normalized Difference Built-up Index

Measures how much built-up, hard surface area is present. Higher values mean more buildings and paved land.
