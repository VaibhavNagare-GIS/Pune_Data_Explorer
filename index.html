Index guide

What the indices measure

NDVI

A normalized difference calculated from near-infrared and red reflectance in the provided workflow.

MNDWI

A modified normalized difference calculated from green and SWIR1 reflectance in the provided workflow.

NDBI

A normalized difference calculated from SWIR1 and near-infrared reflectance in the provided workflow.
